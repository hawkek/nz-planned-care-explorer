# Findings

The three findings on the Overview page are now written by the pipeline
(`R/explorer.R`, `finding_measure()` and `finding_spread()`) from the latest
health target extract, and change with every quarterly update. As generated
from the Q4 2025/26 extract (data to June 2026):

1. **About 66,700 people have waited more than four months for a first specialist assessment.** In June 2026, that was 34% of everyone waiting for a first specialist assessment (about 196,700 people). In January 2019 it was about 10,200 (9%), so the number is now 6.5 times as high. 66% were waiting less than four months, against a health target of 95% by 2030.

2. **About 20,500 people have waited more than four months for planned treatment.** In June 2026, that was 27% to 28% of everyone waiting for planned treatment (about 74,200 people). In January 2019 it was 4,317 to 4,956 (10% to 12%), so the number is now 4.4 times as high. 72% to 73% were waiting less than four months, against a health target of 95% by 2030.

3. **Districts range from 55% to 96% waiting under four months.** In June 2026, the share of people who had waited less than four months for a first specialist assessment ranged from 55% in Southern to 96% in South Canterbury. One of the 20 districts was at or above the 95% health target. Districts provide different mixes of services, so this is a description, not a ranking of performance.

Before quoting any of these at launch or in media material, check them
against the published Health NZ factsheet for the same quarter, and re-read
the wording for neutrality after each update.

An earlier version of this file held hand-written findings from the OIA
release (data to December 2025). Those are superseded: the OIA figures for
January 2025 include the apparent Hawke's Bay double count, and the extracts
run six months later. See the git history for the earlier text.
